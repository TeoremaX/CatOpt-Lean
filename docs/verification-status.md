## Scope of the trusted boundary

Even after Lean compilation, proofs establish only logical properties of the
Lean `Primitives` interface and its Lean interpreters, assuming Lean's
type-checker and the imported Mathlib axioms. They *do not* verify arbitrary
external Python implementations or an operating-system PRNG. The executors
in `experiments/` are not extracted from Lean. Their empirical behavior is
independent and only software-tested.
